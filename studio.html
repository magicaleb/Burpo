Honest working notes

Where it is strongest.

Best material: arrows, letters, bolts, crowns, mountains, simple faces, and abstract angular lines.

Avoid: tiny details, multiple disconnected shapes, handwriting, and more than seven major turns.

Built-in out: reveal the overall geometry first, then call any fine detail an “impression” rather than claiming pixel accuracy.
